# Content brief — ideas from the reference sites

Reviewed 13 Nov 2026. These are **notes on structure and ideas**, not copy to paste.
All final wording must be written fresh for this business, and every fact (prices,
credentials, hours, testimonials) must be the client's own.

## Reference A — taxsolutionsnsw.com.au (Parramatta)

Built on Hostinger Website Builder. Pages: Home, About, Services, Resources, Contact.

**Positioning:** proactive partner, not just compliance — helps clients _understand_
their numbers. Subscription-style pricing with no hidden fees. Audience: sole traders,
startups, family businesses, trusts, companies, SMSFs, individuals.

**Home structure**

1. Top bar with phone + email
2. Hero: firm name, one-line value proposition, "Get Started" button
3. Three pillars: Tax Compliance · Business Advisory · Accounting Services
4. About block: "fundamental services" vs "strategic advisory" lists, why-choose-us
5. Services grid (8 cards)
6. Testimonial with star rating
7. Footer: phones, email, address, enquiry form, PSL disclaimer

**Services (each has a one-line promise + bullet list of inclusions)**

| Service                        | Inclusions                                                                              |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Taxation                       | Income tax, GST, FBT, payroll tax, PAYG, super, CGT — all entity types                  |
| Accounting                     | Financial statements, reconciliations, year-end adjustments, audit support              |
| Tax planning & advisory        | Strategic planning, minimisation, CGT and structuring advice                            |
| Business performance reporting | Budget vs actual, KPIs, cash-flow and cost analysis, forecasting                        |
| Bookkeeping & payroll          | Transaction recording, payroll, fixed asset register, payables/receivables              |
| Data transformation (Xero)     | Setup, file clean-up, historical import, reconciliation, payroll setup                  |
| SMSF administration            | Fund setup, annual reporting, tax return, contributions/pensions, auditor + ATO liaison |
| External CFO — construction    | Job costing, cash-flow forecasting, growth strategy, compliance, KPI tracking           |

**Contact page:** short form (name, email, message), FAQ list (~8 questions), address, weekday hours.

**Resources page:** three placeholder categories (tax management, financial tools,
best practices) and a newsletter signup — no real articles.

**Weak spots to improve on:** duplicated FAQ entries, placeholder-feeling testimonial,
empty Resources section, no individual service pages, no pricing.

## Reference B — intaxpc.com.au (Burwood)

WordPress, very minimal. Pages: Home (banner only), About Us, Services, Prices, Contact Us.

**Positioning:** credentials and value — long-experienced principal, professional
memberships, registered tax agent / SMSF auditor, competitive fees. Specialises in
SMSF and trust work, including audits. Works with MYOB and Xero.

**Useful ideas**

- **About:** leads with qualifications, memberships and years of experience, then a
  "why come to us" paragraph (minimise tax, maximise refund, stay out of trouble).
- **Services (short list):** individual returns · sole trader & partnership ·
  company & trust · SMSF accounting & tax · SMSF & trust account audits ·
  business/company registration.
- **Prices page:** a simple "from $X" list per return type, plus "free quote" and
  "free initial consultation", with a note that prices are a guide.
- **Opening hours with a tax-season variation** (extra Saturday hours Jul–Sep,
  by appointment the rest of the year).
- **Contact:** street address, landline + mobile, email, parking tip.

**Weak spots to improve on:** almost no copy, empty home page, no contact form,
no calls to action.

## What to combine for the new site

| Take from A                                | Take from B                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Home page flow and service pillars         | Credentials-first About page         |
| Service cards with inclusion bullets       | Transparent "from" pricing page      |
| Advisory / CFO / Xero offerings            | Individual-return and audit services |
| FAQ on the contact page                    | Seasonal opening hours, parking info |
| "We help you understand your numbers" tone | "Free first consultation" offer      |

**Add what neither has**

- A page per service (ranks for "SMSF accountant <suburb>" style searches)
- Real articles: EOFY checklist, key ATO dates, deductions by occupation, sole trader vs company
- "What to bring to your appointment" checklist
- Prominent booking / call button on every page
- Google reviews, map, structured data for local search

## Draft service taxonomy

- **Individuals** — tax returns, rental property & investments, capital gains
- **Business** — sole trader/partnership, company & trust returns, BAS/GST, bookkeeping, payroll
- **Super** — SMSF setup, accounting & tax, SMSF audit
- **Advisory** — tax planning, structuring, cash-flow & reporting, external CFO, Xero setup
- **Registrations** — ABN, company, trust, business name

## Tone

Plain Australian English, short sentences, second person ("you"). Confident but no
hype and no promised outcomes. Explain jargon the first time (BAS, SMSF, FBT).
